The Freedman/Gillman History Playlist

The family journeys

Two family worlds run through this journey. The Gillman and Goldberg families trace their roots through Eastern Europe, with the Goldbergs later settling in Blackburn and the Gillman/Rafkin line including Chabad ancestors and relatives whose journeys reached New York, Bluffton, Amarillo and Montevideo. This line also includes the Tumarkin family, among them the last chief Chabad rabbi of Kharkiv. The Gillman and Goldberg families eventually came together in Liverpool.

The Freedman family traces its roots through Vilnius, Trakai and the surrounding towns and villages, among rabbis, shochets, butchers, teachers and tavern landlords, before later generations travelled through Manchester to Blackpool.

Many relatives remained in Eastern Europe. On the Freedman side, family remained in Degsnės and Olkeniki and were murdered at Eišiškės. On the Gillman/Goldberg side, relatives remained across Poland and Belarus; some were murdered at Łódź, while members of the Tumarkin Chabad family were among both those murdered and those who survived.

      About this playlist

      This is a family-history listening journey rather than a comprehensive history of Eastern European Jewry. Items are chosen for reliability, relevance and listenability, not because they represent any particular religious or political viewpoint.
