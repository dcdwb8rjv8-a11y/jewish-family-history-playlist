A personal listening journey

Jon’s Eastern European Jewish family history playlist

Two family worlds run through this journey. Your mum’s maternal Goldbergs belong to the Blackburn story. Her paternal Gillman/Rafkin line includes the Chabad ancestors and descendants who reached New York, Bluffton, Amarillo and Montevideo, as well as the Tumarkin who became the last chief Chabad rabbi of Kharkiv. Bringing the Gillman and Goldberg families together, your mum’s parents eventually settled in Liverpool. Your dad’s Vilnius and Trakai line of rabbis, shochets, butchers, teachers and tavern landlords travelled through Manchester to Blackpool. Of those who stayed behind, your dad’s relatives remained in Degsnės and Olkeniki and were murdered at Eišiškės. Your mum’s relatives remained across Poland and Belarus; some were murdered at Łódź, while members of the Tumarkin Chabad family were among both those murdered and those who survived.
